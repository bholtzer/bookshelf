MaDaFim Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Publisher: BI.H, Israel

TermsDelete account

This policy explains how BI.H (“we”) processes information when you use the MaDaFim Android application and its sharing website.

Information we process

  • Account data: email address, display name, profile image URL, Firebase user identifier and authentication-provider information.
  • Your content: book titles and descriptions, images, PDFs, covers, filenames, page order and other information you choose to upload.
  • Sharing data: invite codes, book-owner and collaborator identifiers, permissions and removal requests.
  • Purchases: subscription product, purchase status and transaction information made available by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Usage and technical data: app interactions, screen views, app-instance/device identifiers, diagnostics, subscription events and approximate location derived from masked IP addresses through Firebase Analytics.
  • Security data: device and app-integrity signals processed through Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity.

Why we use information

We process information to authenticate accounts; save, synchronize and restore books; enable collaboration and invitations; provide subscriptions; secure the service; troubleshoot problems; measure feature use; and comply with legal obligations. Content upload and account data are required for cloud functionality. Analytics supports service improvement.

Storage and service providers

Book metadata is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and uploaded images, PDFs and covers are stored in Google Firebase Storage. Authentication, analytics and integrity services are supplied by Google/Firebase. Purchases are processed by Google Play. These providers process data on our behalf under their applicable terms and may process information outside Israel.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is disclosed to service providers as needed to operate MaDaFim, when required by law, or when you deliberately share a book. A person with a valid invitation may receive access to the shared book and its content. You are responsible for sharing invitations only with intended recipients.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data and cloud content while your account exists or as needed to provide the service. Deleted content may remain briefly in backups, caches or logs before routine removal. Security, fraud-prevention, transaction or legal records may be retained where legally required.

You can permanently delete your account from Account & privacy → Delete account and data. This removes your Firebase account, profile, owned books, pages, covers, uploads and invitations, and removes your access from shared books. If you cannot access the app, use our online deletion page.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. We use authentication, access-control rules and app-integrity checks. No system is completely secure, so users should keep credentials and invitation links private.

Your choices and rights

You may access and edit content in the app, remove individual books/pages, stop sharing, cancel subscriptions through Google Play, or delete your account. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal data by contacting us.

Children

MaDaFim is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service or law changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where required.
